Choose your ground.

Real streets, terrain, and Sentinel-2 ground. Enter coordinates and load the area, or drape your own north-up aerial photo on that same map.

Build stage from aerial photo

Scale is 1:N — 1 is constant. 1:500 is a site plan; 1:3000 is a neighbourhood. The photo is lined up with the Sentinel-2 layer at the coordinates so OSM streets sit on the picture.

Know what’s real

Roads and outlines: OpenStreetMap. Ground slopes: elevation data, where available. The 3D ground uses the newest EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless mosaic we can reach (2025, falling back to 2024–2020 or bundled tiles), or a north-up aerial photo you supply, sized from its 1:N scale and aligned to that satellite layer. Building heights use mapped values or estimates. Doors, windows and foundations are generated, not surveyed. Mapped fences and trees are placed when present; a photo stage also guesses terracotta roofs, tree canopies and grassy yards. No Google imagery is collected. GovMap tiles are not downloaded automatically.

W moves toward your aim; S moves back; A/D strafe relative to your aim. Shift runs; C crouches under low openings; Space jumps larger ledges (small steps are automatic). Click or hold FIRE to shoot. V swaps 2D/3D. N satellite map; M overhead/weapon map; J aim assist; ? control list; F copies a JSON dump of the doorway/floor in front of you (paste it in chat). In 3D, click to capture the mouse and look freely; drag if capture is unavailable. On the AR-15, mouse wheel zooms 3× through the optic; scroll back to hip-fire. In 2D, point to aim; mouse wheel looks up/down. R reloads; Q switches weapon. P previews the iPhone 17 Pro layout. Touch: left stick moves, hold FIRE to shoot, ×3 scopes, ⇄ switches weapon, ↑ jumps (07:00 of FIRE), MAP opens the overhead view.
